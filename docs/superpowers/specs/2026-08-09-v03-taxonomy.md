### 1. `vec(...)` list literal
- **Friction:** models call `vec(3, 8, -2, 12, 7)` as a variadic list
  constructor; Oxide's `vec()` is 0-arity. Dominant OX0303 sub-class in
  ALL THREE families (qwen 69/91, codegemma 59/69, granite 20/27 of
  first-attempt OX0303), arity always matching the task's list length —
  the intent is unambiguous.
- **Evidence:** constrained samples (resolve/OX0303), all families.
- **Class:** sugar (parser/typing accept N args, desugar to the
  push-chain; codegen untouched).
- **Fix:** `vec(a, b, c)` ≡ `push(push(push(vec(), a), b), c)`.
- **Prediction:** first-attempt OX0303 falls hard in all three families
  (order −60/−50/−15); rust arm byte-flat; EX0002 untouched. Some
  freed programs will fail later stages — compile lift smaller than the
  code drop.
- **Design fit:** a list literal spelled through the existing builtin —
  no new semantics, composes with linearity trivially (each push
  consumes and returns). The §53 template exactly.

### 2. Field assignment `s.f = e`
- **Friction:** models assign struct fields in place. The language has
  NO field assignment (verified: `p.x = 5` is OX0101 at HEAD) — only
  functional update `P { x: 5, ..p }`. Under constraint the want
  DEFORMS (`s.f = e` → `s.f == e`, a discarded comparison) into
  downstream OX0300/OX0200 noise; unconstrained it parse-fails.
- **Evidence:** deformation forensics (constrained samples show the
  `==`-fusion signature; the §54 lesson repeating) + unconstrained
  parse failures. Cross-family.
- **Class:** demand-gated FEATURE — needs a SPEC extension before
  implementation (lvalue grammar, resolve/typing of the place, and the
  linear checker's treatment of assigning INTO an owned struct — which
  is standard and safe, but must be pinned).
- **Fix:** extend assignment targets to `lname ("." lname)+`; grammar
  updated in the same commit (parity invariant).
- **Prediction:** the OX0300 Bool-vs-Unit sub-class falls in qwen and
  granite; a share of OX0200-unbound falls; deformation signatures
  vanish from samples. Effect spread across codes rather than one
  counter — declare the sample-level signature the primary check.
- **Design fit:** the language already embraces `let mut` + whole-value
  reassignment; place-assignment into an owned struct is its natural
  completion and linearity-neutral.

### 3. Conversion builtins (`to_int`, `to_str`) and their receiver forms
- **Friction:** models call conversions that don't exist —
  codegemma-dominant (87 of its 179 first-attempt OX0306 are
  `to_int`/`to_string`-shaped receiver calls; qwen 32), plus
  print-adjacent foreign names (`println`, `print_int`: 67 of
  codegemma's OX0200).
- **Evidence:** constrained samples (resolve/OX0306, resolve/OX0200).
- **Class:** builtin additions (Part XI precedent; card row each).
- **Fix:** add `to_str(Int|Float) -> Str` and `to_int(Str) -> Option<Int>`
  (exact set to be pinned in the SPEC amendment; `to_float`/`trunc`
  already exist and stay).
- **Prediction:** OX0306 falls codegemma ≫ qwen > granite — a
  family-ordered dose-response matching the demand counts; `println`
  residue stays (deliberately not added — `print` covers it and the
  card already says so).
- **Design fit:** conversions are the one stdlib hole the corpus's own
  tasks make unavoidable; receiver form comes free via §53.

### 4. Builtin reimplementation / shadowing (`fn len(...)` etc.)
- **Friction:** models defensively re-define builtins as user functions
  → OX0203 duplicate-top-level (dominant sub-class: qwen 103/111,
  granite 109; codegemma's OX0203 is mostly degenerate whole-program
  repetition instead — discount there).
- **Evidence:** constrained samples (resolve/OX0203). CAVEAT: t19+t20
  supply 66% of all OX0203, and 16 degenerate self-repeating programs
  supply 36% of its diagnostics — the count overstates breadth.
- **Class:** design question (allow shadowing? better card line?) —
  card-first: one sentence ("the builtins above are always defined;
  never re-define them") is nearly free and measurable.
- **Fix (this loop):** card wording only. Shadowing semantics is NOT
  proposed.
- **Prediction:** modest OX0203 fall in qwen/granite; codegemma
  largely unmoved (its sub-class is repetition, not intent).
- **Design fit:** teaching beats semantics where intent is defensive
  uncertainty, and the card has ~0 marginal cost under the 900-word cap
  (current headroom must be checked; tighten elsewhere if needed).

### 5. Option ergonomics (`unwrap`, `unwrap_or`)
- **Friction:** models call Option methods that don't exist (qwen 30,
  granite 27 of OX0306); the card teaches `match` and `?`.
- **Class:** builtin addition (`unwrap_or(Option<T>, T) -> T`) — small,
  linearity-clean. `unwrap` (panic) deliberately excluded: the language
  has no panic story to pin, and `?`/`match` cover it.
- **Prediction:** the Option sub-class of OX0306 falls in qwen/granite;
  codegemma (conversion-dominated) barely moves.
- **Design fit:** one total function; no control-flow semantics.

### 6. Instrument: remaining grammar deformations
- **Friction:** three wants still deform under constraint —
  `mut` outside `let` (fuses to `mutn`), `if let Some(x) =` (fuses to
  `letSome(x) ==`), field assignment (dossier 2). A share of
  OX0200-unbound and OX0300 is downstream of these, not independent
  model error.
- **Class:** instrument honesty (the §54 lesson, third instance).
- **Fix:** dossier 2 removes the field-assignment deformation. `mut`
  in for/param position: accept-and-ignore is NOT proposed (unlike
  §54's let-position, these change binding semantics elsewhere);
  instead the card's mutability line gets one clarifying clause.
  `if let`: recorded as demand for v0.4 (feature-class, match sugar) —
  NOT this loop.
- **Prediction:** none this loop beyond dossier 2's.

### Non-candidates, recorded
- **Type-based overloading** (`fn area(Rectangle)` + `fn area(Circle)`,
  15 programs): real demand, feature-class, deferred — enum + match is
  the Oxide answer today; revisit only if it survives the above fixes.
- **`.set(i, v)` index-assign** (qwen-only, 18): low, single-family;
  fold into a future indexing decision, not now.
- **Degenerate repetition & card echo** (granite 44/400 constrained
  echo programs; 282/400 unconstrained builtin-block echoes): model
  competence at this band; no instrument or language action.
